Who qualifies

New Mexico's program is medical and requires a diagnosis

Unlike Colorado, New Mexico limits psilocybin treatment to people with a qualifying condition. This applies to everyone, whether you live in New Mexico or travel from another state. Qualifying diagnoses are:

  • Treatment-resistant depression
  • Substance use disorder, including cannabis, nicotine, or caffeine use disorders
  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  • End-of-life care

If you are not sure whether you qualify, we can talk it through in a free consultation. If you do not have a qualifying diagnosis, my Colorado practice may be the better fit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies for psilocybin treatment in New Mexico?
New Mexico's program is medical. Every participant, in state or out of state, needs a qualifying diagnosis: treatment-resistant depression, a substance use disorder (including cannabis, nicotine, or caffeine use disorders), post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), or end-of-life care. Colorado's program does not have a diagnosis requirement.
When will psilocybin treatment be available in Taos?
New Mexico's program is expected to begin in January 2027. The state is still finalizing its rules, so details on this page may change before then.
How is the New Mexico process different from Colorado?
New Mexico is expected to require a telehealth evaluation appointment to confirm a qualifying diagnosis before treatment begins, and may require a second staff member present during the medicine session. Preparation and integration sessions are part of the process in both states.
How much will psilocybin treatment in New Mexico cost?
Pricing will be set once the state's rules are final. Because of the added evaluation appointment and staffing requirements, New Mexico treatment may cost more than Colorado, where a full course starts at $3,800.
Can I use insurance?
I plan to provide superbills so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement from a commercial insurance plan. Coverage depends on your plan, and I cannot guarantee reimbursement.
How do I get to Taos?
Taos Regional Airport has direct flights from Denver International Airport year-round, and direct flights from Austin, Dallas, and Carlsbad, California, in the winter and summer seasons.
Do you still offer psilocybin facilitation in Denver?
Yes. My Colorado practice at Numia Healing Center in Denver continues. If you do not have a qualifying diagnosis, Colorado's program may be the better fit.

Interested in treatment in Taos?

A free 15-minute consultation is a good first step. We can talk about whether you qualify, what the process will look like, and whether Taos or Denver is the better fit.
