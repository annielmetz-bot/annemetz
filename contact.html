What to expect from a consultation

A free 15-minute consultation is a low-pressure conversation. We'll talk about what you're navigating, whether individual therapy might be a good fit, and what the process looks like. I'll answer your questions honestly — including if I think something isn't a good fit. There's no obligation to move forward.

Request a consultation

This is a request for a consultation, not a booking.

  • Completing this form does not entitle anyone to a consultation. Dr. Metz reserves the right to refuse a request that is not a fit.
  • Dr. Metz reviews each form and proceeds with booking a consultation if it is a fit.
