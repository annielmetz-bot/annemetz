Cookie Settings

Anne Metz LLC · annemetz.com · Last reviewed: November 2026

Short version

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, session-replay, or social-media tools. There is nothing optional to accept or reject here, so you will not see a cookie banner. If that changes, this page and the Privacy Notice will be updated first, and optional tools will stay off until you turn them on.

What this site uses

Tool or storage Provider Category Purpose Duration
Page hosting GitHub Pages Necessary Delivers the pages. GitHub's servers see your IP address and browser type in ordinary server logs for security and operation. No cookie is set on annemetz.com. Server log only
Web fonts Google Fonts Necessary Loads the typefaces used on the site. Google receives your IP address and browser information when a page loads. Google states it does not set cookies for font delivery. None (no cookie)
Consultation request form Formspree Necessary Processes the form on the Request a Consultation page and sends the submission to me. Formspree runs spam protection when you submit and may set a cookie on its own domain (formspree.io) during submission, not on annemetz.com. Submission only

What this site does not use

  • Analytics (no Google Analytics or similar)
  • Advertising pixels or remarketing tags
  • Session recording or replay
  • Embedded video, maps, chat widgets, or social-media buttons
  • Local storage or any first-party cookie
  • Tracking on the consultation form or any page that could reveal health interests

Browser signals and your controls

Because no optional tools run, there is nothing to opt out of. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or similar opt-out signal, it is honored by default. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings; doing so will not affect this site. For help, or if you believe a tracking tool is running that is not listed here, contact privacy@annemetz.com. Please do not include health information in that message.

Pages that link elsewhere

Links to other websites, including thirtysixthparallel.com and the Google booking page used to schedule consultations, take you to services with their own cookie practices and notices.
