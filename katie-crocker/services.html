Services

End-to-end benefits management tailored to your organization

Core Services

Everything Your Benefits Program Needs

Whether you need ongoing management or help with a specific project, I offer flexible engagements to meet you where you are.

  • ✓

    Health, Dental & Vision Plan Design

    Evaluate, design, and implement medical, dental, and vision insurance plans. I work with multiple carriers to ensure you get the best coverage at the best price — including PPO, HMO, HDHP/HSA, and self-funded options.

  • ✓

    Retirement Plan Management (401k / 403b)

    Administer retirement savings plans including contribution structures, employer match strategy, vesting schedules, loan policies, and plan amendments. I coordinate with plan recordkeepers and advisors to keep your plan competitive and compliant.

  • ✓

    Open Enrollment Planning & Execution

    Full-service open enrollment management: timeline creation, carrier coordination, employee communications, benefits fairs, HRIS configuration, and post-enrollment auditing. No more enrollment chaos.

  • ✓

    ACA, ERISA & COBRA Compliance

    Stay compliant with federal and state benefits regulations. Services include ACA employer mandate tracking, Form 1094/1095 reporting, COBRA administration, SPD and wrap document preparation, and 5500 filing coordination.

  • ✓

    Voluntary & Supplemental Benefits

    Expand your benefits package with life insurance, short- and long-term disability, accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, and pet insurance — at little or no cost to you.

  • ✓

    Wellness Programs

    Design and implement employee wellness initiatives — including EAP programs, mental health benefits, gym reimbursements, and health incentive programs — that reduce claims and improve morale.

  • ✓

    Benefits Benchmarking & Competitive Analysis

    Analyze your total compensation package against industry peers and regional competitors to identify gaps and opportunities that affect your ability to attract and retain talent.

  • ✓

    Employee Benefits Communication & Education

    Develop clear, compelling benefits guides, FAQs, videos, and presentations that help employees make informed decisions and appreciate the full value of their package.

  • ✓

    HRIS Benefits Module Implementation

    Configure and optimize the benefits administration module in your HR information system — whether that's Workday, ADP, BambooHR, Rippling, or another platform.

  • ✓

    New Hire Benefits Onboarding

    Create seamless benefits enrollment experiences for new employees — including automated workflows, deadline reminders, and one-on-one Q&A support.
