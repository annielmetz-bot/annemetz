Medical psilocybin treatment in Taos, New Mexico

New Mexico's Medical Psilocybin Program is expected to begin in January 2027. Once certified, I plan to offer treatment in Taos for adults with a qualifying diagnosis, with a particular focus on the women I have long worked with. My Denver practice continues alongside it.

Why Taos

A place suited to this work

Where you do this work matters. Psilocybin heightens your awareness of your surroundings, and the days after a medicine session are when insight settles in. Taos offers a setting that a city office cannot.

In nature

Taos sits at the foot of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains, between the Carson National Forest and the Rio Grande Gorge. Step outside after a session and you see mountains, open sky, and high desert. There are few better places to begin integration than a walk into that landscape. At night the sky is dark and full of stars, and Taos is seeking Dark Sky certification.

Slower and quieter

Taos is a small town. There is less noise, less traffic, and fewer demands on your attention, which leaves room to rest and reflect before and after your medicine day.

A long spiritual history

Taos Pueblo has been home to the Tiwa-speaking people for more than a thousand years. For the past century, artists, writers, and people looking for meaning have come here for the land and the light. Many people feel that history when they arrive.

A reason to stay longer

Many people extend their trip. Taos Ski Valley is about 35 minutes from town. In summer there is hiking, rafting on the Rio Grande, and hot springs. Time outdoors in the days after a session supports the work.

Who qualifies

New Mexico's program is medical and requires a diagnosis

Unlike Colorado, New Mexico limits psilocybin treatment to people with a qualifying condition. This applies to everyone, whether you live in New Mexico or travel from another state. Qualifying diagnoses are:

  • Treatment-resistant depression
  • Substance use disorder, including cannabis or nicotine use disorders
  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
  • End-of-life care

If you are not sure whether you qualify, we can talk it through in a free consultation. If you do not have a qualifying diagnosis, my Colorado practice may be the better fit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies for psilocybin treatment in New Mexico?
New Mexico's program is medical. Every participant, in state or out of state, needs a qualifying diagnosis: treatment-resistant depression, a substance use disorder (including cannabis or nicotine use disorders), post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), or end-of-life care. Colorado's program does not have a diagnosis requirement.
When will psilocybin treatment be available in Taos?
New Mexico's program is expected to begin in January 2027. The state is still finalizing its rules, so details on this page may change before then.
How is the New Mexico process different from Colorado?
New Mexico is expected to require a telehealth evaluation appointment to confirm a qualifying diagnosis before treatment begins, and may require a second staff member present during the medicine session. Preparation and integration sessions are part of the process in both states.
How much will psilocybin treatment in New Mexico cost?
Pricing will be set once the state's rules are final. Because of the added evaluation appointment and staffing requirements, New Mexico treatment may cost more than Colorado, where a full course starts at $3,800.
Can I use insurance?
I plan to provide superbills so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement from a commercial insurance plan. Coverage depends on your plan, and I cannot guarantee reimbursement. Because this is a medical program, the intake evaluation, preparation, and integration sessions are medically necessary health care appointments and are coded that way on your superbill.
How do I get to Taos?
Taos Regional Airport has direct flights from Denver International Airport year-round, and direct flights from Austin, Dallas, and Carlsbad, California, in the winter and summer seasons.
Do you still offer psilocybin facilitation in Denver?
Yes. My Colorado practice at Numia Healing Center in Denver continues. If you do not have a qualifying diagnosis, Colorado's program may be the better fit.
