Legal Psilocybin Facilitation · Colorado

A licensed therapist who brings clinical depth to every stage of psilocybin work

I provide psilocybin facilitation through Colorado's regulated natural medicine program at Numia Healing Center in Denver. Unlike some facilitators who focus only on the medicine session itself, I bring decades of clinical therapeutic training to preparation, the experience, and integration.

You are in control throughout this process. My role is to help you prepare with intention, hold steady presence during the experience, and translate what emerges into real, lasting change in your life.

This work is especially powerful for women navigating hormonal, reproductive, and identity transitions — when the terrain is shifting and clarity feels out of reach.

About Dr. Anne Metz

Therapist first. Psilocybin facilitator second. Always both.

I'm a licensed professional counselor with a PhD in counselor education — not someone who took a weekend course. My clinical training spans years of practice in trauma, grief, life transitions, and complex mental health work. That foundation is what I bring to every psilocybin session.

I'm also Director of Fluence Training's natural medicine program in Colorado, serve on New Mexico's Medical Psilocybin Patient Safety Subcommittee, and host The Psychedelic Skeptic podcast — where I bring rigorous scrutiny to a field that often deserves it.

I work with women because women's experiences — in bodies, in time, in grief — are too often undertreated and underestimated.
