What this practice is

Psilocybin facilitation for women at significant thresholds

Women come to this work carrying many different things — grief, a body that feels unfamiliar, a sense of being stuck, a question about who they are becoming. What they have in common is that the ordinary tools haven't been enough. Psilocybin, with skilled facilitation, can reach what other approaches cannot.

The question "who am I now?" deserves more than coping strategies. Empty nesting, divorce, aging, the end of a chapter — psilocybin is particularly well-suited to the identity and meaning work that major transitions require.

Why a women's practice?

Working with a woman isn't just a preference — it changes what's possible

Psilocybin surfaces what lives in the deep interior — grief, fear, shame, longing, the stories we hold about ourselves as women. Who is in the room with you during that experience is not incidental. It shapes what you feel safe enough to meet.

Women navigating hormonal transitions, reproductive losses, or identity questions carry a particular kind of material. The felt sense of being truly understood by someone who inhabits the same landscape — not just clinically familiar with it — creates a quality of safety that allows for deeper work.

Anne brings both: clinical expertise in women's psychology and the lived understanding of a woman who has navigated her own thresholds.

About Dr. Anne Metz

Therapist first. Psilocybin facilitator second. Always both.

FAQ

Common questions

Is psilocybin facilitation legal in Colorado?
Yes. Colorado voters passed Proposition 122 in 2022, creating a regulated program for the supervised therapeutic use of psilocybin. Sessions take place at state-licensed healing centers with state-licensed facilitators. I hold Colorado Natural Medicine Facilitator License No. NMIT.0000254.
Who is this work for?
I work with women navigating significant thresholds — perimenopause and menopause, infertility and reproductive grief, other grief and loss, identity questions, and major life transitions. I'm a licensed professional counselor with a PhD in counselor education, so every stage of the work is clinically grounded.
Do I have to live in Colorado?
No. The full-day medicine session must take place in person in Colorado, and clients travel from anywhere for it. Preparation and integration sessions are available to anyone via telehealth. New Mexico facilitation is expected to begin in January 2027.
How much does psilocybin facilitation cost?
A full course of facilitation — two preparation sessions, one full-day medicine session at Numia Healing Center in Denver, and two integration sessions — starts at $3,500, including access to the licensed healing center. Insurance is not accepted for facilitation services.
