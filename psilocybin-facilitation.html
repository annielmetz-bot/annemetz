Session 3 · The Medicine Day

What the medicine session actually looks like

The medicine administration session is a full day — typically six to eight hours. You arrive at Numia Healing Center in Denver, take the psilocybin in a safe, comfortable room, and rest. Eyeshades and music are provided. You are lying down for most of the experience.

I am with you the entire time. My role is not to guide or direct — it is to hold steady, safe presence so you can follow the experience wherever it goes. If something difficult arises, I am there. If you need grounding, I am there. You are never alone.

The experience itself is internal — the room is simply the container that makes it possible to go deep. What happens inside that container is yours.

The Science

How psilocybin-assisted therapy works

Understanding the mechanism helps you approach the experience with informed intention.

The biological mechanism

Psilocybin works primarily by binding to serotonin 5-HT2A receptors, promoting neuroplasticity — increasing connectivity between brain regions that don't typically communicate. This allows the brain to form new patterns and break free from rigid loops associated with depression, anxiety, and trauma.

Research also suggests psilocybin temporarily quiets the default mode network — the brain network responsible for our sense of self, rumination, and repetitive thinking. This creates genuine space to step outside the mental loops that keep us stuck.

The experiential dimension

At therapeutic doses, psilocybin can shift perception in ways that allow you to see yourself, your relationships, and your life from a fundamentally different vantage point. Psychological defenses soften. The mind surfaces what most needs attention — through imagery, emotion, memory, or direct insight.

This combination of biological and experiential change is why psilocybin-assisted therapy is so promising — especially for women navigating the identity and meaning dimensions of major life transitions.

Colorado Natural Medicine Program

Is psilocybin legal in Colorado?

Yes. Colorado voters passed Proposition 122 in November 2022, creating a regulated framework for the therapeutic use of natural medicines including psilocybin. The state licenses facilitators, healing centers, and the cultivation and testing of psilocybin products.

This is not recreational legalization. Colorado's program is structured around supervised, therapeutic use. Sessions take place at licensed healing centers with trained, state-licensed facilitators. There are no dispensaries and no take-home products. Every step — from screening to preparation to administration to integration — is designed to prioritize safety and therapeutic benefit.

No. Psilocybin is not appropriate for everyone.
