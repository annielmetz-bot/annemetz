My Story

I didn't set out to become a benefits expert — I stumbled into it as an HR generalist who got handed a benefits renewal packet and a stack of confusing vendor proposals. Within a year, I'd renegotiated the company's health plan, cut premiums by 14%, and realized I'd found my calling.

That was over twelve years ago. Since then, I've guided benefits programs at companies spanning every industry — technology firms scaling through hypergrowth, established manufacturers navigating union negotiations, nonprofits stretching every dollar, and healthcare organizations managing uniquely complex compliance landscapes.

Today, I work as an independent benefits manager and consultant, partnering with HR teams and business owners who need an expert in their corner — someone who can think strategically about benefits design, negotiate directly with carriers, and handle the administrative details that too often fall through the cracks.

My Approach

Benefits aren't just a line item on a P&L statement. They're a signal to every current and prospective employee about how much you value them. That conviction shapes everything I do.

1

Listen Before I Recommend

Every organization has a different workforce, culture, and financial reality. I start with a thorough discovery process — learning your goals, pain points, demographics, and budget constraints before suggesting a single change.

2

Data-Driven Plan Design

I use industry benchmarking data, utilization analytics, and claims history to design benefits packages that deliver real value — not just impressive-sounding plan names. Better data means better decisions for your employees and your budget.

3

Carrier Negotiations on Your Behalf

Insurance carriers negotiate more favorably with experienced advocates. I've built relationships across the major carriers and know how to structure a compelling case for better rates and plan design concessions.

4

Educate Your Employees

The best benefits package in the world is wasted if employees don't understand it. I develop communication materials, host enrollment workshops, and create reference guides that make benefits genuinely accessible to every member of your team.

What I Believe

After more than a decade in this field, a few principles have become non-negotiable for me.

Transparency First

I explain every recommendation in plain language — no jargon, no hidden agendas. You should always understand exactly what you're buying and why.

Compliance Is Non-Negotiable

Regulatory missteps are expensive and damaging. I treat compliance as a foundation, not an afterthought, and stay current on every change that affects your plans.

Employees Are the Client

Even when the company writes the check, the people using these benefits are the true end users. I keep their experience and wellbeing at the center of every design decision.

Long-Term Thinking Wins

I'm not interested in quick fixes that create problems down the road. Every recommendation is made with multi-year sustainability in mind — for your budget and your people.
