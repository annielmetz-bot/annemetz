Preparation and integration counseling · New Mexico & Telehealth

Counseling before and after a psilocybin experience

Preparation and integration sessions are ordinary counseling. They give you a place to get ready for an experience, and to make sense of it afterward and bring what you learned into daily life.

What to expect

Counseling, not administration

Preparation

We talk through your reasons for considering an experience, your history, your questions and worries, and the support you have around you. Preparation is a counseling conversation. It is not a medical clearance.

Integration

After an experience, we work on what came up: feelings, memories, insights, and questions. The aim is to understand it in the context of your life and decide what, if anything, you want to do with it.

Ongoing counseling

You can also work with me on anything else that brings you to counseling. Sessions follow your goals and your pace, and no outcome is promised.

A clear line

Anne Metz LLC does not provide psilocybin

My counseling practice, Anne Metz LLC, provides counseling only. I do not supply, recommend a source for, or administer psilocybin or any other controlled substance in these sessions, and I cannot help you find one.

Psilocybin administration is offered only by Thirty-Sixth Parallel LLC, a separate company that is not part of Anne Metz LLC. Its services, records, and billing are kept apart from my counseling practice. Working with me for counseling does not obligate you to use that company, and the reverse is also true.

Psilocybin is regulated differently from state to state, and some of those programs are still being put in place. Whether a state program is available to you is not something I can promise or decide for you.

How it works

Where and how sessions happen

Telehealth

Video sessions are available for clients located in New Mexico, Colorado, Oregon, California, and Virginia. Before the first session I confirm where you are physically located for each visit, and I complete intake and licensing requirements for your state.

In person

Occasional in-person sessions are offered in Taos, New Mexico. I share the location when you book.

I accept New Mexico Medicaid for clients located in New Mexico. Care for clients in other states is self-pay. You can ask for a Good Faith Estimate of self-pay charges before you schedule.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide psilocybin?
No. Anne Metz LLC provides counseling only. Psilocybin administration is offered only by Thirty-Sixth Parallel LLC, a separate company.
Do I need to be planning an experience to see you?
No. Some clients are preparing, some are integrating an experience from the past, and some come for counseling without any connection to psilocybin.
Can you tell me whether psilocybin is safe or right for me?
No. I am a counselor, not a prescriber, and I cannot provide medical clearance. I can help you think through your questions and encourage you to talk with your own medical provider about your health history and medications.
How do I get started?
Request a free 15-minute consultation. This is a request, not a booking. I review each request and, if it is a fit, schedule a consultation. Please do not include health details in the form.
