Core Services

A Full Spectrum of Benefits Expertise

Whether you need strategic guidance, hands-on administration, or regulatory compliance support, I offer flexible engagement models to fit your needs and budget.

■

Health Insurance Plan Design

Your health benefits are your most visible commitment to employee wellbeing — and often your largest non-payroll expense. I help you strike the right balance between comprehensive coverage and cost control, working with you to evaluate carrier options, structure plan tiers, and negotiate the best possible rates.

✓ Medical, dental, and vision plan evaluation
✓ Carrier RFP management and negotiation
✓ PPO, HMO, HDHP, and self-funded plan design
✓ Supplemental benefits (life, disability, critical illness)
✓ FSA, HSA, and HRA account administration
✓ Annual renewal analysis and plan comparison
▲

Retirement Plan Administration

A strong retirement benefit is one of the most powerful tools for attracting experienced talent and rewarding long-term employees. I help you select, implement, and manage retirement plans that are competitive, compliant, and easy for employees to understand and use.

✓ 401(k), SIMPLE IRA, and SEP-IRA plan setup
✓ Plan document review and maintenance
✓ Employer match structure and vesting design
✓ ERISA fiduciary compliance oversight
✓ Recordkeeper and TPA vendor management
✓ Annual compliance testing coordination (ADP/ACP)
●

Open Enrollment Management

Open enrollment is one of the most complex and time-sensitive events in the HR calendar. A poorly managed enrollment causes confusion, increases errors, and damages employee trust. I take ownership of the entire process — from planning and communications to platform configuration and issue resolution.

✓ Enrollment timeline planning and project management
✓ Benefits portal and platform configuration
✓ Employee communication campaign development
✓ Live and virtual enrollment meeting facilitation
✓ Decision-support tools and benefits comparison guides
✓ Carrier eligibility file management and reconciliation
■

Compliance & Regulatory Management

The regulatory environment surrounding employee benefits is complex and constantly evolving. A single compliance misstep can result in significant penalties and employee harm. I keep your benefits program on the right side of the law — proactively, not reactively.

✓ ACA reporting (Forms 1094/1095) and employer mandate compliance
✓ COBRA administration and notice management
✓ HIPAA privacy and portability compliance
✓ FMLA and leave administration coordination
✓ Summary Plan Description (SPD) preparation
✓ DOL and IRS audit preparation and response
▲

Employee Education & Communication

Even the most generous benefits package fails if employees don't understand how to use it. I develop communication strategies and educational materials that make benefits feel accessible and valuable — boosting utilization, satisfaction, and overall program ROI.

✓ Benefits guide and handbook development
✓ New hire benefits orientation programs
✓ Year-round benefits communication calendar
✓ Wellness program promotion and engagement
✓ Retirement readiness workshops
✓ Benefits intranet and FAQ content development
●

Benefits Benchmarking & Strategy

Are your benefits competitive enough to attract the talent you need? I conduct thorough benchmarking analyses against industry peers and geographic competitors, then translate the findings into an actionable roadmap for improving your total compensation package.

✓ Industry and geographic benchmarking analysis
✓ Total compensation statements for employees
✓ Benefits cost modeling and budget forecasting
✓ Competitive gap analysis and recommendations
✓ Multi-year benefits strategy development
✓ Executive benefits and deferred compensation review
My Process

How We Work Together

Every engagement follows a structured process designed to surface the right insights, build the right program, and deliver a seamless experience for your team.

1

Discovery & Assessment

We start with an in-depth review of your current benefits, workforce demographics, budget, and goals. I ask the questions most consultants skip.

2

Strategy & Design

Based on discovery findings, I develop a tailored benefits strategy and plan design recommendations — with benchmarking data to back every suggestion.

3

Implementation

I manage the full implementation — carrier setup, platform configuration, employee communication, and enrollment — so nothing falls through the cracks.

4

Ongoing Management

Benefits don't run themselves. I provide ongoing administration, compliance monitoring, and annual renewal management to keep your program running smoothly.
