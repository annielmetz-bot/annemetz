My Story

Passionate About Benefits That Make a Difference

I've spent my entire career focused on one thing: making sure employees have access to the benefits they need — and that organizations can afford to provide them.

After earning my degree in Human Resources Management and spending a decade in corporate HR roles, I launched my independent consulting practice to bring enterprise-level benefits expertise to organizations of every size.

I've seen firsthand how the right benefits package can transform a workplace culture — reducing turnover, improving productivity, and making employees feel genuinely valued. I've also seen what happens when benefits are mismanaged: compliance penalties, disengaged employees, and rising costs with diminishing returns.

My approach is straightforward: understand your organization deeply, build a strategy that aligns with your goals and budget, and execute flawlessly.

My Approach
